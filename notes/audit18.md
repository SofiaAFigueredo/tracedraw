## #611 (partly)

What the attachments show: A 50 s screen recording (2818x1536, h264). ffmpeg decoded it at 1 fps into 50 frames, plus full-size stills at 23, 31, 38, 41, 43, 45, 46, 47 and 49 s. Along the black/yellow boundaries there is an olive/brown band of blobs. Many thin white crescents and hairline slivers sit inside the black stripes and along the black/yellow edge, where the white page shows through between regions. There are faint lighter outlines inside the yellow, and the antenna edges are fuzzy with white flecks.

- The white hairline gaps between neighbouring colour regions (41-49 s) are not addressed. outline() traces each palette colour from its own exact mask (apps/tracedraw/src/trace.rs:185) and fits each ring on its own (trace.rs:200, fit_ring). The fitting tolerance is up to 0.6 px at Clipart detail 60 (trace.rs:355). A ring without corners is cut into quarters from wherever its own contour happens to start (trace.rs:361). So a hole in one region and the region that fills it get different curves. Each region covers only its own pixels and the background is removed by default, so the page shows through. Stacking dark to light (trace.rs:175) does not hide this, because no region reaches under its neighbours.
- Thin anti-aliasing bands between two colours that are not the background survive. despeckle (trace.rs:225) merges patches by pixel count only; speck_area (trace.rs:217) is 16 px at the Quick Trace default, Clipart detail 60. A strip of an in-between colour that is 1-2 px wide but long, like the olive band along the black/yellow boundary at 49 s, stays as its own sliver region. Issue item 02 (colour bleeding around high-contrast edges) is only partly covered.
- Extra near-duplicate colour regions remain. Quick Trace defaults to Clipart with 32 colours (trace.rs:61). On flat art with about 7 colours, median cut spends the other entries on edge shades and noise, which gives the faint outlines inside the yellow and two near-blacks seen in the video. PR #717 left 'merging regions that differ by a hair in colour' for later, and nothing on main does it. Settings.merge_adjacent (trace.rs:87) exists but nothing reads it.
- No test covers the video's case. The only #611 test (trace.rs:1095) uses the LineArt preset: black and white, one region plus background. There is no test with two touching colours that are not the background and have anti-aliased edges, and nothing checks that the rendered trace shows no page between regions.

Follow-up: In trace.rs outline(): (a) Make neighbouring regions share their boundaries. Either fit each boundary chain between two colours once and reuse it reversed for the other region, or stack the regions so that each lower region's mask also covers the pixels of the regions drawn above it. Either way, no page can show between them. (b) Extend despeckle so that patches at most about 2 px thick (gone after a 1 px erosion) merge into the neighbouring colour they touch most. Also merge palette entries that are very close, or that only lie on edges between two other colours, before tracing. (c) Add a regression test: an anti-aliased yellow disc crossed by a black band on white, Clipart preset. Render the trace with render_traced (trace.rs:944) and assert that no white or transparent pixel appears inside the original shapes and that the region count is small.
